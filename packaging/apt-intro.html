dnsmasq, packaged for Debian from upstream's master and Debian's own packaging, with one change: streaming AXFR (RFC 5936), so dnsmasq can serve authoritative zones larger than 64 KB to secondary nameservers. The package names are Debian's, so these replace Debian's dnsmasq. Built from the packaging branch of mithro/dnsmasq; see its packaging/README.md.
